# Privacy settings

Goal: the TV stops collecting viewing data and showing promotions.

**Verification status:** Paths compiled from secondary sources, labels differ by model and firmware. Confirm each switch on your own TV.

## Switches to turn off

| Where (varies) | Setting | Target |
|----------------|---------|--------|
| General, System, Additional Settings | Live Plus (automatic content recognition) | Off |
| Home Settings | Home Promotion | Off |
| Home Settings | Content Recommendation | Off |
| Additional Settings, Advertisement | Limit AD Tracking | On |
| General, User Agreements | Viewing Information | Off |
| General, User Agreements | Voice Information | Off |

## Why this is not enough alone

These switches reduce what the TV does, but a software setting is a promise by the vendor. The robust measure is to give the TV no internet at all. See [Network isolation](network-isolation.md).
